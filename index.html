About the project

UPI has changed how India pays, but many people still do not recognise common scam tricks until it is too late. Our chatbot lets a person describe a suspicious situation and tells them, in plain language, whether it is likely a scam.

It covers 17 common fraud scenarios and offers AI-powered guidance for suspicious situations not listed in the menu.
